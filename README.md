# InLegalLaySum: A Novel Benchmark for Lay Summarization of Indian Legal Documents

Long Paper accepted at the **AACL-IJCNLP 2026 (Findings)**!

## InLegalLaySum Dataset

InLegalLaySum  (**In**dian **Legal** **Lay** **Sum**marization), a dataset comprising 9,570 judgements from the Supreme Court and several High Courts of India, paired with expert-written summaries in English. 

The full dataset can be found under https://huggingface.co/datasets/trishitasaha/InLegalLaySum . 

**Folder Structure -**

**InLegalLaySum_Train** 

It contains total **7470** samples. Each sample subdirectory contains 3 txt files -- Judgement.txt, Expert_Summary.txt and Simplified_Summary.txt  where Judgement.txt contains the original case judgment, Expert_Summary.txt is the summary of the judgement written by law practitioner and Simplified_Summary.txt is the lay summary generated by Gemini-2.0-Flash.

**InLegalLaySum_Test**

It contains total **2100** samples. Each sample subdirectory contains 2 txt files -- Judgement.txt and Expert_Summary.txt where Judgement.txt contains the original case judgment, Expert_Summary.txt is the summary of the judgement written by law practitioner.
